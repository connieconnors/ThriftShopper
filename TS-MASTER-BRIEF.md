Users are not driven by financial profit maximization; they are driven by the search for a "good home" and narrative preservation for things they once loved. The platform acts as an emotional closure mechanism to help people detach from possessions and pass them on safely.

### The Ecosystem Flywheel & Data Interconnectivity
GoShed's frictionless, voice-first capture layer serves as the ultimate intake engine. When a user captures an object's history to let it go, the AI handles the translation layer, simultaneously creating:
1. An immutable asset page in the **MemexMe / My Trove Book** legacy archive.
2. A frictionless, auto-styled peer-to-peer listing in the **ThriftShopper** marketplace.
